### 🎓 Glad to see you here!

<h3 align="center">Postdoctoral Research Fellow | National University of Singapore (NUS) | Materialyze.AI Lab</h3>

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Research Fellow** at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**, working with **Prof. Shyue Ping Ong** in the **Materialyze.AI Lab**.

Previously, I completed my PhD at the **Indian Institute of Technology Indore** under the guidance of **Prof. Biswarup Pathak**, where I was a **Prime Minister’s Research Fellow (PMRF)**.

My research lies at the intersection of **computational materials science**, **quantum mechanics**, and **machine learning**, with a focus on accelerating the discovery and design of advanced materials. At NUS I work on **foundation potentials** for high-entropy alloys in fusion and aerospace as part of the **AI Foundry** programme.

---

### 🔬 Research Focus

* 🧠 **Machine-learning interatomic potentials & foundation models** (MACE, CHGNet, MatterSim, M3GNet, TensorNet/QET): benchmarking, fine-tuning and distillation
* 🔍 **Explainable AI**: bonding-aware descriptors, SHAP and symbolic regression for design rules
* 🧬 **Generative AI & Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)** for inverse materials design (CrystaLLM, CGCNN, ALIGNN)
* 🔥 **Anharmonic phonon transport**, ultralow and high **lattice thermal conductivity (κₗ)** and **thermoelectrics**
* ⚛️ **High-throughput, descriptor-based screening** of electrocatalysts (ORR, OER, HER, NO₃RR, CO₂RR)
* 🛡️ **Alloys for fusion and extreme environments**: H/He in refractory high-entropy alloys
